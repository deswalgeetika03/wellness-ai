# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in the Wellness AI project to be respectful, safe, and constructive for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and respectful in discussions and reviews.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Share accurate information and acknowledge uncertainty, especially about health and safety claims.
- Respect privacy. Do not post another person's private information or sensitive health details without permission.
- Accept reasonable, constructive feedback.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable. Deliberately misleading health or safety claims presented as verified project results are also out of bounds.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and project-related communications.

Project maintainers will review reports, protect the privacy of people involved as far as practical, and may edit or remove content or restrict participation. To report a concern, contact the repository owner through the contact options on the GitHub profile for `deswalgeetika03`. For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of posting publicly.

This project is maintained on a best-effort basis; reports may not receive an immediate response.
